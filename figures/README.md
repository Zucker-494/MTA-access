# JTG figure set

All figures in this directory are 300 dpi PNG files generated from the corrected frozen analysis.

## Main-text figures

Manuscript numbering follows order of appearance:

- **Figure 1:** `fig2_metric_gap.png` — annual MTA-reported availability and calendar continuity.
- **Figure 2:** `fig1_spatial.png` — mutually exclusive spatial diagnostic categories for long-observed station complexes.
- **Figure 3:** `fig3_reclassification.png` — sorted station-complex recurrence differences; bar positions are non-overlapping.
- **Figure 4:** `fig4_persistence.png` — adjacent-year persistence of MTA-reported availability rankings.
- **Figure 5:** `fig5_concordance.png` — monthly Kendall concordance of borough rankings from asset and accessible-platform metrics.

## Supplementary figures

- `figS1_management_coefficients.png` — exploratory management coefficients with 95% confidence intervals.
- `figS2_calendar_persistence.png` — adjacent-year persistence of calendar-continuity rankings.

## Overlap control

The revised graphics avoid label-on-data and point-on-point overplotting. The spatial map uses mutually exclusive categories, a reduced base-marker size, a legend outside the mapped area, a north arrow, and a 5 km scale bar. Figure 3 uses ordered bars rather than a labelled scatter cloud. Figure 5 uses one monthly concordance series rather than 420 overplotted borough-month points.

Station coordinates are sourced directly from MTA Subway Stations and Complexes (dataset 5f5g-n3cz). Borough boundaries are sourced from NYC Department of City Planning Borough Boundaries (dataset gthc-hcne).
